# Facts the assistant may state
- 1930 is India's national helpline for reporting online financial fraud. Call as soon as possible after money is lost. Police of the state or UT handle the calls. A call can help hold money if it is still traceable. It does not reverse a transfer, and recovery is not guaranteed.
- Official portal: cybercrime.gov.in.
- After calling 1930 the caller gets an SMS with an acknowledgement number. Keep it and use it on the portal.
- The helpline typically asks for: mobile number, bank or app, account or UPI ID, transaction ID and date, amount, a short description, screenshots.
- Contact your bank only through official channels (number on your card, official app or website), never numbers from messages.
- Transaction ID: for UPI it is usually a 12-digit number. Find it in the payment app history, the bank SMS or the statement. Other payment modes use other formats.
- Never share an OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, password or CVV with anyone. Do not pay anyone offering to recover your money. Callers posing as officials who demand money to avoid arrest are scammers.
- 112 is the emergency number (only when out of scope).
Never state: operating hours of 1930; recovery chances or timelines; what a specific bank will do; legal outcomes; that a complaint was filed by this prototype.
